# Consumer Desktop UX

## Desktop shell
- Max content width: ~1440px.
- Persistent top bar: RENUABL wordmark left; Home / Energy / Support or minimal contextual navigation; account right.
- During guided purchase flow, replace generic nav emphasis with progress navigation.
- Use 12-column layout; large breathing room; avoid full-width cards everywhere.

## Recommended desktop pattern
Left rail or compact progress rail + primary content + optional contextual insight panel.

### Guided flow layout
- Left: steps and completion state.
- Centre: active task.
- Right: contextual confidence layer (house image, outcome card, testimonial, or mascot) only when it earns its space.

## Screen behaviour
### Home
Use a clean split layout:
- headline + address + Ask RENUABL
- mascot / abstract energy object
No traditional solar nav grid.

### Energy profile
Questions are large cards/toggles with generous spacing.
Do not place every question above the fold if it makes the screen feel dense.

### Recommendation
Show the system as one coherent recommendation, not a catalogue.
Technical details live behind disclosure.

### Installer match
One matched installer card, centred and confident.
“Why we matched them” collapsible detail.
Alternative installers secondary.

### Date booking
Calendar and time-window selector can sit side by side.
Selected date summary remains visible.

### Checkout
Two-column layout:
- left: order summary
- right: deposit/payment card
No aggressive ecommerce banners.

### My RENUABL
This may become dashboard-like, but keep it consumer-simple:
- Today
- Energy
- Insights
- System health
- Upgrade
- Support
Prioritise narrative insights over raw graphs.
