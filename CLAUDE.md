- Sizing (`recommendSystem`), explained to customers under "How we worked this out" on the system step (`sizingExplanation`; keep it in step): Essential = the least solar that covers the home's yearly use at the year's average sunshine, never below the 5 kW minimum system (`ASSUMPTIONS.minSystemKw`); wanting a battery doesn't change it. Rule of thumb (`solarDay`): the home uses `ASSUMPTIONS.baseSelfConsumption` (40%, PLACEHOLDER; 30–40% is typical) of its solar as it's made, never more than its daily use; the rest of its use is after dark; the 60% surplus charges the battery, then exports. With a battery (Recommended, Maximum) it's sized for winter (`winterYield`: NASA's June–August sunshine against the year, else `ASSUMPTIONS.winterSunShare` 0.5): `panelsForBattery` fills the smallest hybrid inverter to `COSTING.maxArrayToHybridWithBattery` (150%, PLACEHOLDER until the CER/network wording is confirmed with Primero; 133% without a battery) whose winter surplus covers the after-dark use; the battery (`batteryForSolar`) is the smallest 8 kWh-module size whose 90% usable holds that winter after-dark use (one size up for backup). Savings (`estimateOutcome`) use the same `solarDay` at the year's average. Customers never choose panel counts or battery sizes. The options are Essential = solar only, Recommended = solar + battery, Maximum = the same solar and battery + a smart EV charger (the EV charger is only in Maximum; customers can still switch it on or off on the system step). Expanding existing solar: a smaller battery / the battery / the battery + EV charger (battery sized to the bill's after-dark purchases, `batteryNeeded`; top-up panels use `batteryReadySolar` headroom).

## Architecture rules

- Business logic lives in `src/lib/domain` (pure TS, unit tested). Don't put it in components.
- UI reads and writes only through `src/lib/services/*`; mock data stays in `src/lib/mock`.
- Use the semantic color tokens (`bg-canvas`, `bg-surface`, `text-ink`, `text-muted`, `border-line`, `text-positive`, …) rather than raw colors so both themes work.
- `Card` drops its white `bg-surface` when its className has its own `bg-…` (otherwise the white wins and e.g. white text on `bg-forest` disappears).
- `cn` only joins classes (no tailwind-merge): a `className` can't override a component's own display or colour classes, so wrap it (e.g. a `hidden lg:block` div) instead. Disabled buttons use solid muted colours, never transparency.
- Small text links and controls get a ~44px touch area with the `tap-area` utility (in `globals.css`). The mobile menu (`MobileMenu`) offers "Start with your address" and "My RENUABL", plus a quieter "Become a partner" (for installers and retailers). The home page header also has "For solar businesses" (to `/business`, RENUABL Business), home page only, never mid-purchase. In preview, partner sign-up offers a sample certificate of currency (`public/samples/`) for testing; the live server rejects it.
- Typing boxes are at least 16px on touch screens (`@media (pointer: coarse)` in `globals.css`): smaller text makes iPhone zoom in on tap and stay zoomed, so the page slides sideways. Keep it that way for any new field.
- Pages never scroll sideways on phones: `html, body` have `overflow-x: clip` (clip, not hidden, so sticky headers keep working), and decorations that hang past the edge (the home hero's `.sun-glow`) sit in an `overflow-x-clip` container. Check new pages at 390 px: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` must equal the screen width.
- Mobile-first, but build deliberate desktop layouts (see `FlowStep`, `InstallerShell`) rather than stretching mobile.
- Don't put `position: fixed` elements inside an animated (`transform`) ancestor, because it breaks fixed positioning. `FlowStep` renders its sticky CTA outside the animated section for this reason.
- Server pages that depend on "today" call `await connection()` so they render per request.
