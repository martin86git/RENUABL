The menu as it is today.

1 · Frosted glass

See-through white, blurred behind, a bright hairline along its top edge and a soft lift off the page. Today and the pen round get a gentle curve of light so they read as pressed glass beads.

2 · Liquid glass

The most lively: a sheen across the top half, a rim of light all round, deeper lift. The pen is a lit orb, matching Sol's sun at the other end. Hover a word to see it catch the light.

3 · Warm glass

Frosted, with a breath of the signal orange coming up through it and a warm halo round the pen. Ties the bar to Sol and the designer without adding colour to the words.

The second row of each shows the glass over something with colour behind it (a page scrolled under the bar). Over a plain near-white page, glass shows mostly through its edges, light and lift.
